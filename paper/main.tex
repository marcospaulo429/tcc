\begin{abstract}
LLM agents interleave decisions from two layers: the \emph{model} (tool calls)
and the \emph{harness} (context management, retries, termination). We show, via
deterministic counterfactual replay, that per-decision causal contributions
$C(H)$, $C(M)$ and their interaction $I(H,M) = C(H,M) - C(H) - C(M)$ are
measurable above a measured noise floor, identify two interaction regimes
(screening-off and synergy), and demonstrate that single-layer credit
over-credits shielded decisions --- a bias corrected by interaction-aware
credit during harness training. \todo{números finais}
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Contributions.}
(1) Deterministic replay infrastructure with measured noise floor (exact-match
fidelity); (2) per-decision counterfactual decomposition $C(H)$, $C(M)$,
$I(H,M)$ on the same trajectory; (3) anatomy of interaction: screening-off
(forced model action shields the harness decision, $C_{HM}=C_M \Rightarrow
I=-C_H$) vs.\ synergy; (4) control tasks showing the method distinguishes
harmless from harmful harness decisions by construction; (5) interaction-aware
credit improves harness training vs.\ outcome-only and pure $C(H)$,
dose-matched in total rollouts. \todo{ajustar pós-gates}
