# Figures and tables of the paper (as compiled from `paper/main.tex`)

All figures are generated by `paper/figs.py` (`.venv/bin/python paper/figs.py`)
from serialized artifacts in `runs/` and `experiments/results/`; every count
drawn is asserted against the corresponding `report.json` before saving.
Main-text figures are sized for a single ICLR column (width ≤ 5.5 in).
